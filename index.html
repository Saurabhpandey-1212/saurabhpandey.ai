About Me

From spreadsheets to strategy.

My career began in MIS and data — building reports for banking clients, then training systems for government programs. That foundation taught me something most account managers learn late: the numbers always tell the story first.

At GreenHonchos, I turned that data instinct into business impact — starting as an MIS Operations Executive designing dashboards for Sales, Performance Marketing, SEO, and Marketplace teams, then earning four promotions in three years to lead E-Commerce delivery and account management for a portfolio of 20+ premium D2C and GCC brands.

Today I sit at the intersection of client relationships, operations, and AI automation — running business reviews with brand leadership, orchestrating platform integrations across Technology, Marketing, and Supply Chain, and building Claude Code & Codex-powered workflows that have cut operational effort by roughly 40%.
